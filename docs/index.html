What is the Levelling Up Fund?

The Levelling Up Fund (LUF) was a UK government capital fund, launched in 2021, worth £4.8 billion. It paid for local infrastructure — town-centre regeneration, transport, and cultural or heritage assets — intended to reduce economic gaps between richer and poorer parts of the UK, the agenda the government called "levelling up".

Money was awarded by competitive bidding: local authorities submitted project proposals, and winners were announced in Round 1 (October 2021) and Round 2 (January 2023). Each award funds a specific, named project rather than general council spending.

Official evaluations have found that many funded projects are running behind their original timelines — but those are one-off manual snapshots. This tracker focuses on the 27 projects led by North West England councils across Rounds 1 and 2, and infers each one's current delivery status from public sources, so the picture can be refreshed on demand.
